❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is WhaleTrack?
WhaleTrack is a free tool that tracks the biggest traders (whales) on Polymarket, the world's largest prediction market. It shows their open positions, recent bets, and win rates in real time so you can follow smart money.
What is Polymarket?
Polymarket is a decentralized prediction market where people bet real money on the outcomes of real-world events — politics, sports, crypto, and more. It runs on the Polygon blockchain and has processed over $1 billion in trading volume.
Who are the biggest Polymarket whales?
The top Polymarket whales tracked by WhaleTrack include traders like somalianKing, bettguy, CandleHammerDrums, coldsway, and DEEDDIT. These wallets regularly place bets of $10,000–$100,000+ on single markets and have strong historical win rates.
How do I track Polymarket whale bets?
Visit whaletrack.app to see real-time whale positions on Polymarket. You can also subscribe to free Telegram alerts at @WhaleTrackPolybot to get notified the moment a whale places a large bet — up to 10 minutes before it appears on Twitter.
Is WhaleTrack free?
Yes. WhaleTrack is completely free to use. The website, leaderboard, and Telegram bot (@WhaleTrackPolybot) are all free. There is no subscription required to track whale bets on Polymarket.
How does copy trading work on Polymarket?
Copy trading on Polymarket means placing the same bet as a top whale trader. WhaleTrack shows each whale's open positions with a direct "Copy Bet" button linking to Polymarket. You choose the amount and place the trade yourself.
How often is WhaleTrack data updated?
WhaleTrack refreshes whale position data every 5 minutes. The Telegram bot fires alerts within 60 seconds of a whale placing a bet over $15,000.
