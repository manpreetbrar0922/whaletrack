Risks of Copy Trading on Polymarket

⚠️ Important: Copy trading is not risk-free. Past performance does not guarantee future results on prediction markets.
  • Slippage risk: If you copy a whale too late, the market price may have already moved. Real-time alerts reduce this window.
  • Position size mismatch: Whales bet $10,000–$100,000. You don't need to match them — scale to your own bankroll.
  • Market resolution risk: Even 65% win-rate traders lose 35% of the time. Diversify across multiple positions.
  • Whale strategy changes: A whale who dominates political markets may lose edge in other categories. Follow their strongest specialty.
