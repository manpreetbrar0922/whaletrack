How the Polymarket Leaderboard Works

Polymarket calculates leaderboard rankings using the following metrics:

  • Profit & Loss (P&L): Total USDC profit after subtracting losses. The primary ranking metric.
  • Trading Volume: Total USDC traded across all markets. High volume traders appear near the top even with moderate win rates.
  • Win Rate: Percentage of positions closed profitably. Top leaderboard traders typically maintain 55–70% win rates.
  • Markets Traded: Number of distinct prediction markets entered. Diversification can reduce risk and improve leaderboard rankings over time.
