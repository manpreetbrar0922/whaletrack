Why Copy Whale Trades?

The logic is simple: whales have already done the research. When someone with a documented 70% win rate bets $30,000 on an outcome, that's a signal worth paying attention to.

Copy trading on prediction markets works differently from crypto or stocks. On Polymarket:

  • Markets resolve to 100% or 0% — there's no middle ground
  • Whale positions are fully public on-chain
  • You can enter the same market at similar prices
  • Win rates are calculable from on-chain history

This means you can track exactly how good a whale is before deciding to copy them. No guessing.
